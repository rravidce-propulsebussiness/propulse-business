## How a construction project works

1. **Plan and consult**: discuss the plot, built-up area, budget and goals.
2. **Design and specify**: align drawings, materials, finishes and scope of work.
3. **Build and review**: agree milestones, quality checks and project communication.
4. **Handover**: review the completed scope, documents and agreed handover items.

## What to share for an estimate

Plot dimensions and location, planned built-up area, number of floors, drawings if available, preferred specifications, budget and expected timeline.
