Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

ProPulse is used to capture enquiries from Meta lead forms and make lead access available to businesses through the ProPulse marketplace. This policy explains what information is processed for that service and when it is shared.

Information we process

Depending on the connected Meta Page, form and fields selected by its owner, information may include:

  • Meta account, Page and lead-form identifiers and names, plus connection and permission details.
  • Answers a person submits on a lead form, such as name, phone number, email address, location, project needs and other form responses.
  • Lead and advertising details such as lead ID, ad ID, ad or campaign name, platform and Meta-reported ad spend used to calculate lead pricing.
  • Basic technical, security and activity records needed to operate and troubleshoot the service.

How we use information

We use this information to connect a business-selected Meta Page and lead form, retrieve and organize enquiries, prevent duplicate imports, route leads in the marketplace, calculate lead prices using reported ad spend and the configured markup, and provide the lead access a business purchases. Meta access is used for lead retrieval and related read-only ad-spend reporting; ProPulse does not use it to create or edit advertising campaigns.

When information is shared

When lead access is purchased, the lead details are made available to the business or businesses authorized to access that lead through the marketplace, so they can respond to the enquiry. We also share information with service providers that host, secure or operate ProPulse, and with Meta as needed for the connection. We do not publish connected lead lists for public access or provide them to general data brokers.

A business that connects a form is responsible for giving people an appropriate privacy notice and choices on that form, including notice that their enquiry may be shared with a business that purchases lead access. People who submit a form should review that form’s notice and contact the business that published it about the enquiry.

Meta connection and security

The connecting business chooses which Page and form to connect and can disconnect that connection in ProPulse. OAuth access tokens are encrypted at rest. Disconnecting stops future imports from that connection; it does not automatically remove leads already imported or previously shared with a buyer.

Retention and privacy requests

We retain connection, lead and transaction records for as long as needed to operate the service, provide marketplace access, maintain support and transaction records, protect security, and meet applicable obligations. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information by emailing info@propulsetechnologies.online. Deletion may not remove records a buyer has already received or records that must be retained. A buyer may need to handle a request for information held in its own systems.
