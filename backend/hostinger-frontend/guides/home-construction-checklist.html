Home construction checklist: plot to handover

Use a stage-by-stage checklist so important decisions are made before work begins rather than during expensive site changes.

A practical home construction checklist covering site inputs, design, soil and structure, quotations, materials, execution, waterproofing, quality checks and handover.

Start your requirement · Find professionals

01

Before design

Collect plot dimensions, survey information, access constraints, ownership/approval inputs, utility information and your family’s room/parking/future-floor requirements.

02

Design and engineering

Freeze architectural layouts, structural design, service routes and major elevations before comparing execution quotations.

03

Quotation comparison

Check scope, quantities/measurement method, material allowances, exclusions, taxes, milestones, timeline assumptions and variation rules.

04

Structure and masonry

Use approved drawings and stage inspections for reinforcement, formwork, concrete, masonry openings and service sleeves before work is concealed.

05

Waterproofing and services

Plan wet areas, roofs, balconies, plumbing pressure tests, electrical conduits and drainage before finishes.

06

Finishes and handover

Approve samples, snag rooms systematically, test services, collect warranties/manuals, document changes and close pending items before final payment.
