Plumbing checklist for a new house

Good plumbing planning starts before slab sleeves, wall chases and toilet levels are fixed. Coordinate water, drainage, waterproofing and maintenance access as one system.

Plan water supply, drainage, toilets, kitchen, utility, tank/pump systems, sleeves, testing and maintenance access before finishes close the walls and floors.

Start your requirement · Find professionals

01

Freeze fixture locations early

WC, basin, shower, kitchen sink, washing machine, dishwasher and outdoor taps affect pipe routes and drainage levels.

02

Coordinate sleeves and vertical shafts

Plan service shafts and slab/wall penetrations before structural and masonry work is closed.

03

Separate water and drainage decisions

Supply pressure/storage, hot water, rainwater and soil/waste drainage each need coordinated sizing, routing and access.

04

Test before concealment

Pressure/leak testing and drainage checks should happen before tiles, ceilings or boxing make repairs difficult.

05

Keep valves and cleanouts accessible

Future maintenance should not require unnecessary demolition. Mark concealed routes and retain as-built information.
