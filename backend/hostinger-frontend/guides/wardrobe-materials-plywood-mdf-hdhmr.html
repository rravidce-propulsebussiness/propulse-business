Plywood vs MDF vs HDHMR: what should you use for wardrobes?

There is no universal “best board” for every wardrobe. Select the substrate based on moisture exposure, finish type, hardware, machining requirements, budget and the interior specification.

Compare common wardrobe substrate choices such as plywood, MDF and HDHMR by moisture exposure, finish, screw holding, machining and project-specific use.

Start your requirement · Find professionals

01

Separate substrate from surface finish

The internal board and the visible finish are different decisions. A laminate, veneer, acrylic or painted finish can be applied over different suitable substrates.

02

Consider moisture exposure

Wardrobes near damp external walls, wet areas or poor ventilation need more attention to moisture-resistant detailing and building dampness.

03

Check screw holding and hardware use

Heavy shutters, pull-outs and repeated hardware loads can make edge quality, density and fixing method important.

04

Match the board to the finish process

Painted routed shutters, laminate shutters and veneer work can favour different substrates and fabrication methods.

05

Ask for exact brand/grade/thickness in the quotation

Avoid generic words like “premium board.” Write substrate type, grade, thickness, finish, edge treatment and hardware range into the scope.
