Six checks before hiring a construction company

1. Verify the company and accountable team

Confirm legal business name, address, relevant registrations and tax details where applicable, named project contact and site supervisor. Match the quotation issuer, contracting entity and payment recipient.

  • Legal identity and relevant registrations
  • Named project manager and site supervisor
  • Written responsibility for defects and variations

2. Inspect comparable completed projects

Ask for projects similar to your plot, building type, floor count, finish level and budget. Photographs alone are not proof; request a permitted visit and customer reference.

  • Multiple completed projects
  • Clarify subcontracted work
  • Ask about defects and handover experience

3. Check drawings and engineering responsibility

The home must be designed for its actual plot, soil, loads, spans and intended use. Structural design and reinforcement details must come from qualified professionals.

  • Architectural drawings and revisions
  • Soil investigation where required
  • Structural drawings and design responsibility
  • MEP coordination

4. Compare the BOQ, not only the rate

Compare identical drawings and specifications. Check measurement basis, quantities, material standards, taxes, provisional items and exclusions.

  • Foundation, RCC, masonry, plaster and waterproofing
  • Steel, cement and concrete specifications
  • Electrical, plumbing, windows, flooring and painting
  • Taxes, external works and variation rules

5. Inspect quality control during execution

Ask how materials are stored, who checks reinforcement before concreting, how concrete is placed and cured, and how waterproofing is tested.

  • Pre-pour inspection and engineer sign-off
  • Concrete and curing records as specified
  • Waterproofing test records
  • Stage-wise inspection notes

6. Put payments, schedule and warranty in writing

Tie payments to verifiable progress. Document milestones, substitutions, delay handling, defect reporting, retention if agreed and handover.

  • Milestone-linked payment schedule
  • Written variation process
  • Completion timeline
  • Defect-liability and warranty terms
  • Handover checklist

Compare quotations fairly

Share the same plot, built-up area, floors, drawings, structural scope and material specifications. Compare exclusions and taxes as carefully as the headline price. There is no universal ranking that proves one company is best for every homeowner.

Frequently asked questions

Which is the number one construction company in Hyderabad?

There is no universal, independently verified number-one builder for every project. The right choice depends on project type, location, technical scope, budget, delivery record and contract terms.

What should a house construction quotation include?

Drawings, specifications, measurement basis, material standards, labour scope, taxes, exclusions, provisional items, payment milestones, timeline and variation procedure.

Is a per-square-foot rate enough to compare builders?

No. Compare the same built-up-area basis, structural scope, materials, finishes, services, taxes and exclusions.

Should I pay the full construction amount in advance?

Avoid paying the full amount upfront. Agree a written milestone schedule tied to measurable progress and documented checks.
